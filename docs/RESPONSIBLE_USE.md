# Responsible Use

AI Influencer Media Grabber is a local tool. You decide what it downloads, edits and
generates, and you are responsible for how you use the results. This page explains the
rules that apply and the notices the app shows.

## Downloading content

- Download only content you own, content you have permission to use, or content the
  law lets you use (for example, private study or fair use / fair dealing where it
  applies). Being able to download something does not mean you may re-upload it.
- Respect each platform's terms of service. Many platforms do not allow downloading
  or automated access, and accounts can be rate-limited or suspended.
- Keep credits: the app saves the source link next to each download so you can
  attribute the creator.
- Private, login-only and age-restricted posts need your own logged-in session
  (Firefox cookies or an imported `cookies.txt`). Only use your own account, and never
  share your cookies file - it gives access to your account.
- The app shows a short download and copyright notice the first time you download.

## Face swap, lip-sync and likeness

Face swap, lip-sync and portrait animation change what a real person appears to say or
do. Before using them:

- **Get consent.** Only use the face, voice or likeness of yourself or of people who
  have clearly agreed to this use.
- **Do not deceive or harm.** Never create content that impersonates someone, misleads
  viewers about real events, harasses, sexualises or defames anyone, or targets minors.
  In many countries this is illegal.
- **Label synthetic media.** Disclose AI-edited or AI-generated content where viewers
  could mistake it for real footage, and follow each platform's labelling rules.
- **Check model licenses.** Some face models (InsightFace `inswapper_128` and
  `buffalo_l`, CodeFormer) are for non-commercial use only. The app marks them
  "Personal / non-commercial use only" and asks you to accept their license before
  downloading.

The app shows a consent and likeness notice the first time you use face swap or
lip-sync.

## AI Cleaner (metadata rewriting)

The AI Cleaner can remove AI-generation metadata and write camera-style (for example
iPhone) EXIF data into images. This feature is opt-in. Be aware that:

- Presenting AI-generated or edited media as an unedited camera photo can break
  platform rules on synthetic or manipulated media and can mislead your audience.
- Some platforms and jurisdictions require AI content to be disclosed. Removing
  provenance data does not remove that obligation.
- Use it for legitimate purposes such as privacy (removing location or device data)
  or consistent formatting - not to deceive.

## Cloud features

Cloud actions (fal.ai, Google Gemini) are never used automatically. They send the
selected media to that provider under its terms, use your own API key, and may cost
credits. The app shows the provider and a cost confirmation before submitting. See
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Reporting problems

If you find a way the app could be misused, or a notice that is unclear, please open an
issue on the project's GitHub page.
